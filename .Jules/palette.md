## 2026-07-13 - [Accessibility & Feedback for Non-Standard/Custom Anchor Actions]
**Learning:** Anchor tags without `href` attributes are omitted from the default keyboard focus/tab order, making them completely inaccessible to keyboard and screen-reader users. Additionally, when triggers simulate progress over multiple clicks, the lack of incremental/narrative visual and screen-reader feedback makes the transition feel broken or unresponsive. Adding progressive state announcements and ARIA attributes (`role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`, `role="button"`) transforms opaque click sequences into accessible interactive steps.
**Action:** Always provide an `href="#"` (along with `event.preventDefault()`) or use button elements for trigger actions, implement a distinct focus indicator, apply `role="button"`, and use dynamic `aria-live` containers for progressive action feedback.
